UF

A family kitchen that grew into a neighbourhood regular

My father always said a restaurant should feel like a dinner at home. When we opened our doors in Kashinath Patil Wadi, we kept that rule: warm food, enough on the plate, and a table that never feels rushed.

I still remember the first week. A few neighbours came in for tikka, and the next evening they brought their parents. Since then, birthdays, office lunches and Sunday dinners have all found a seat here.
